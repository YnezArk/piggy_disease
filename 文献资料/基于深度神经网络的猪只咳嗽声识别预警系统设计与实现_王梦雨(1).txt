DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION OF PIG COUGH SOUND
 RECOGNITION AND EARLY WARNING SYSTEM BASED

                    ON DEEP NEURAL NETWORK

                                       ABSTRACT

      Livestock farming is the pillar industry of China's agriculture. Modern livestock and
poultry breeding industry is developing towards scale, intensification and automation. As
an important part of livestock and poultry breeding industry, pig breeding affects the
national economy and the people's livelihood. In the precision breeding of pigs, respiratory
diseases that affect the production capacity of pigs are difficult to prevent and cure, and
spreads rapidly, such as lung inflammation, wheezing, and so on. Most importantly, cough
is one of the main early symptoms of respiratory diseases, how to quickly monitor the
cough sound of pigs and give early warning of respiratory diseases is one of the hot issues
in modern pig breeding industry.

      At present, the health monitoring of pigs in modern farms mainly depends on
experienced breeders to judge their health status according to their vocalization and
postures. which is labor-intensive, strong subjective, missed inspection and untimely
inspection. Meanwhile, it is inefficient to monitor the prevalence of cough in pigs by
artificial methods, which will cause varying degrees of stress response in pigs and have an
adverse impact on the subsequent diagnosis and treatment of pig diseases. With the
advantages of non-contact and rapid response, acoustic signal recognition technology is
more and more used in the field of precision breeding, such as monitoring the physiological
growth of animals in livestock and poultry breeding. Therefor, this paper studied and
designed a practical pig cough sound recognition and warning system, so as to provide
theoretical basis and technical support for the early judgment of pig respiratory diseases in
pig welfare breeding.

      In this paper, the pig cough recognition model based on deep neural network can
effectively distinguish the pig cough sounds and other interference noises after
preprocessing and extracting the characteristic parameters. At the same time, combined
with the model, the pig cough sound recognition and warning system platform was
developed. The main research contents and conclusions include:

                                                                                                                                                 III
                                    

      (1) Construction of isolated word database of pig cough recognition system: The
audio collection environment was two isolated areas with a length of 2.4m, a width of 1.6m
and a height of 0.9m in the piggery. The corpus was constructed from 5 healthy pigs and 5
sick pigs aged 2 to 3 months with the body weight of 50 ± 5kg. Firstly, the audio processing
software Adobe Audition (AU) was used to intercept the sound segment of recorded data in
the way of manual marking. A total of 536 original pig sound samples including cough,
snore, songs and sneeze were intercepted. After comparing different noise reduction and
endpoint detection methods, the final corpus was obtained by spectral subtraction denoising
and double-threshold endpoint detection. There were 1058 cough sound samples and 420
other sound samples, including 184 sneezing sounds, 121 songs and 115 snoring sounds.
The number of validation set and test set was respectively set to 10% of the number of
sound samples of this type. Samples were randomly selected for each experiment and the
remaining samples were set as training sets.

      (2) Characteristic parameters (logFBank, MFCC) extraction of pig sound signal:
By comparing and analyzing the time domain and frequency domain characteristic
diagrams of the four kinds of sounds, it was found that there were obvious differences in
the spectrograms of different sound signals. In this paper the pre-emphasis coefficient was
set as 0.97, the sampling frequency was set as 48KHz, the frame length was set as 25ms,
and the frame overlap was set as 10ms, the 64-dimension filter bank (Log_filter Bank,
logFBank) and 128-dimension Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficents (MFCC) features of the
four sound signals were extracted respectively, with the combination of first-order and
second-order differences as the input of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and
Feedforward Sequential Memory Neural Networks (DFSMN), the binary classification and
quad-classification recognition training of the two models were carried out.

      (3) Research on classification and recognition model of pig cough sound based on
deep neural networks (CNNs, DFSMN): The three-dimensional array constructed by
combining the logFBank and MFCC features of the extracted four kinds of sound signals
with their first-order and second-order variances was used as the input of CNNs and
DFSMN models. A total of four models were realized for the two classification recognition
of pig cough and other sounds and the four classification recognition of different sounds,
the effects of different feature extraction methods and different iterations on the model were
compared. Experimental results showed that the CNNs model with MFCC as the feature
input had better effect to recognize pig cough sound, and the recognition accuracy of cough

sound in the test set was 97%, recall rate was 96%, F1-score was 98%, and the combined
average accuracy was 96.71%.

      (4) Design and implementation of pig cough recognition and early warning
system: Using the intermediate software to obtain the audio data stream of the pickup in
real time and save the audio to the folder of the deep learning machine. The deep learning
algorithm was used to segment a large section of audio to meet the call conditions of pig
cough recognition model. The cough recognition results were stored in MySQL database by
invoking the model. At the same time, Springboot + Vue + Ngnix was used to build the
software platform of pig cough recognition and early warning system to realize the
functions of pig house, pig information display in the client software and pig cough early
warning. This system can provide convenient and efficient management system for
breeding personnel, and improve breeding production efficiency.
