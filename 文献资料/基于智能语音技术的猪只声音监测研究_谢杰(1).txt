   Research of Pig Sound Monitoring Based on Intelligent Voice
                                   Technology

                                             JieXie
                                  Directed by Bing Zhou

                                     Abstract

      Pork has become one of the most important meat grains in China, playing a vital role in
stabilising the rice bowls of the Chinese people. Against the background of a steady increase
in demand for pig production, China's pig breeding industry model is developing in the
direction of scale, intensification and modernisation, which, at the same time, also brings
some new challenges. For example, there are phenomena such as reduced pig breeding space,
greater breeding density and poor ventilation in the process of large-scale and intensive
farming, which undoubtedly intensifies the risk of pigs suffering from respiratory diseases and
most likely leads to a concentrated outbreak of pig diseases in serious cases. The detection
and prevention of respiratory diseases in intensive pig farms is crucial, and research shows
that coughing is one of the early clinical signs of respiratory diseases, therefore, the study of
how to monitor the sound of pigs to warn pigs of diseases is one of the key issues and hot
issues in modern pig farming. The traditional method of monitoring pigs' coughs is to
determine the health status of pigs and the risk of disease outbreaks, which is time-consuming,
labour-intensive, inefficient and prone to zoonotic diseases and other conditions that affect the
normal growth of pigs.

      This paper first investigates and analyses the current status of research on pig sound
monitoring at home and abroad, and carries out research on pig sound classification using

                                                                          III
                                       

data collection, data pre-processing, feature extraction, model building and optimization as
the technical route, as follows:

      (1) The collection and processing of pig sound data. Under the guidance of farming
professionals, the original samples were labeled and cut, and 10,000 target samples were
extracted from over 100,000 original data. The target samples included five types of sounds:
eating, drinking, grunting, grunting and coughing. The target samples were pre-processed
with pre-emphasis, frame addition and filtering to reduce noise. For the background noise in
the pig sound samples, the noise reduction effects of spectral subtraction, Wiener filtering and
wavelet analysis were compared, and Wiener filtering was selected for noise reduction. The
FIR filter was used for noise reduction of specific noises in the pig samples.

      (2) The parameters of pig sound are extracted. In this study, four types of feature
parameters were extracted from the pig sound signal, including short-time energy and short-
time transient rate in the time domain, power spectral density in the frequency domain, speech
spectrogram in the time-frequency domain, and Mel's cepstrum coefficient in the cepstrum
domain. The MFCC was used as the input data for the classification model.

      (3) A study is conducted on pig sound classification models. In this study, four pig sound
classification models were constructed based on five types of pig sound data sets: support
vector machine model (SVM), hidden Markov model (HMM), long and short-term memory
network (LSTM) and improved long and short-term memory network (LSTMP), and the
accuracy rate was selected as the model effect evaluation index. The results of the study
showed that the overall classification accuracy of SVM, HMM, LSTM and LSTMP for pig
sounds was 87.83%, 87.65%, 90.49% and 91.54% respectively, which all reached above 85%,
and the improved model LSTMP based on LSTM was higher than the classification accuracy
of LSTM itself, and the improved model improved the classification effect by about This
indicates that the classification models used in the study are all feasible and effective for
classifying pig sounds, and that the LSTMP has improved somewhat over the base model
LSTM.

      (4) Optimization of pig sound classification models. In this study, three intelligent
optimization algorithms, whale optimization (WOA), particle swarm optimization (PSO) and

                                                                          IV
                                       

quantum flower pollination optimization (QFPA), were adopted to build three optimization
combination models, WOA-LSTMP, PSO-LSTMP and QFPA-LSTMP, and the research
results showed that WOA-LSTMP, PSO-LSTMP and QFPA-LSTMP were more accurate than
The classification accuracies of WOA-LSTMP, PSO-LSTMP and QFPA-LSTMP were
92.54%, 92.77% and 93.47% respectively, and all three optimised combination models were
higher than the classification accuracy of LSTMP model, which proved that the three models
optimisation methods were feasible and effective, among which QFPA-LSTMP had the best
classification accuracy.

      A visual display of pig sound classification based on MATLAB GUI was designed,
combined with the above pig sound classification research results, laying the foundation for
online real-time monitoring of pig sound in the later stage. New ideas for pig disease early
warning are provided by the research results, and the smart farming technology can be
improved, and the healthy development of the pig farming industry can be digitally
empowered.

      Keywords: pig sounds; data pre-processing; feature extraction; classification models;

epidemic early warning;
