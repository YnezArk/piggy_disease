Research on automatic detection method of pig cough 
sound based on multi-classifier  fusion in a complex 
environment  
Abstract 
 
Respiratory disease is one of the most common  of diseases in intensive pig breeding mode. Pig 
respiratory diseases have the characteristics of strong infectivity and high mortality. If they cannot 
be detected in time, it is easy to cause large -scale infection and seriously affect economic benefits. 
The traditional way of manual inspection is ineff icient and prone to missed detection. With the help 
of contact less acoustic intelligent perception technology, monitoring the sound in the pig house and 
recognizing the cough sound of pigs can realize the ear ly warning of pig respiratory diseases, which 
is of great significance to improve pig production and realize healthy pig breeding.  
The sound environment of the real pig house is very complex, including the interference s of 
environmental control equipment, feeding machines and other strong noises. At the same time, 
multiple pig screams  and pig coughs may be overlapped , which brings great challenges to high -
precision pig cough detection. At present, most of the existing methods base on manually segmented  
single sound dataset, and do not consider the complex situations such as the overlap of multiple 
sounds. Although good classification results are achieved, there is still a certain gap between  them 
and the detection results in the real pig house environment. To solve this problem, this paper studied 
the pig cough automatic detection method based on multi -classifier fusion in  a complex environment, 
established a continuous sound dataset in a complex environment, and studied the multiple voice 
activity detection algorithm based on adaptive dynamic threshold and the classification algorithm 
based on multi -classifier fusion for the complexity of pig house environment. The main work of this 
paper is as follows : 
(1) A continuous sound dataset in a complex piggery environment is established , including 
continuous cough segments and continuous noncough segments.  There are 316 continuous cough 
segments, 7 -40 seconds per segment, and 25 continuous noncough segments, 10 minutes per 
segment.  The dataset contains a total of 3408 coughs and 3133 noncoughs. With the help of 
veterinarians , the onset and offset  of all coughs in the dataset are annotated. Finally, the time -
frequency features of this dataset are statistically an alyzed, and the complexity of the data is 
explained. This dataset provides data support for subsequent research.  
(2) A multiple voice activity detection  algorithm based on adaptive dynamic threshold is 
constructed. The noise environment of the real pig house is complex and changeable, and the sound 
overlap often occurs, which leads to the obvious limitations of the traditional voice activity detection  
 
 
 IV 
method. In this paper, a new voice activity detection  algorithm is proposed, which can adaptively 
adjust the number of voice activity detection  and its threshold value according to the specific 
complex situation, the signal to noise ratio of single voice segment s and the duration of single voice 
segment s. The experimental results show that the proposed method achieves a cough detection rate 
of 97.6%, which is significantly higher than that of the traditional voice activity detection  method 
of 75.8%.  
(3) The pig cough sound recognition method based on multi -classifier fusion is constructed . 
Considering the complexity of cough sound data and the imbalance of noncough sound data, the 
data augmentation  method is studied to increase the diversity of data. The data is augment ed from 
the perspectives of sound and image features. The results show that the performance  of sound data 
augmentation  and image data augmentation  are close, and the performance  of noise addition is the 
best, which can improve the accuracy by about 1%.  To solve the problem of sound classification in 
a complex environment, a c lassification model based on multi -classifier fusion is constructed to 
improve the classification accuracy. The key to multi -classifier fusion method  is to construct a set 
of base classifier s with good performance and certain diversity. In this paper, multiple base 
classifier s are constructed by studying the feature s of multiple different domains and multiple 
different types of classification models. A multi -classifier selection algorithm based on accuracy -
diversity metrics is proposed to optimize the selec tion of all base classifiers and select the optimal 
base classifier fusion scheme. The experimental results show that through data augmentation  and 
multi-classifier fusion , the classification accuracy of the model in a complex environment can be 
effectively improved, and the accuracy can reach 96.0%.  Finally, for the results of automatic 
detection of pig cough sound, a continuous cough judgment algorithm is proposed to realize the 
recognition of continuous cough.  
In summary, this paper constructs a multiple voice activity detection  algorithm based on 
adaptive dynamic threshold to achieve high -precision automatic detection and segmentation of the 
vocal segment in continuous sound, and constructs a multi -classifier fusion algorithm to achieve 
high-precision cough classification in a complex environment . This study provides technical means 
for the automatic detection of pig cough in the real pig house environment, and provides effective 
monitoring means and technical s olutions for healthy pig breeding.  
 
Key words:  Pig, Cough sound recognition , Audio detection , Ensemble  learning , Healthy breeding
